This memo supplies prices and accounting formulas. It does not establish the
size, security, throughput, or latency of any garbled-RAM construction. The
intended workload is 10 parties, malicious security with abort, 10,000 queries/s,
32-byte keys and 32-byte values, with N=2^28 or N=2^32. SSD performance is
assumed separately provisioned so that party communication remains the target
bottleneck; SSD capacity cost is retained. CPU/preprocessing work remains a
separate feasibility constraint, never a free resource.

3. FULL-DUPLEX NETWORK ACCOUNTING -- VERIFIED EXPLICITLY
AWS states that advertised bandwidth is available to inbound and outbound
traffic simultaneously. Consequently use max(tx,rx) for a symmetric instance's
capacity constraint, not tx+rx. Both directions must individually fit.
For asymmetric party roles, compute each party's transmit and receive demand
separately. An aggregator/evaluator may have much more receive traffic than
the average party. Network byte totals count each send once for billing.
Single TCP/UDP flows normally cap at 5 Gbps; a cluster placement group can
raise that to 10 Gbps, and eligible same-AZ ENA Express flows to 25 Gbps.
Use enough flows and all relevant NICs. These are capacity specifications,
not measured application throughput; packet/connection allowances can bind.
https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/ec2-instance-network-bandwidth.html

Use the requested model factors consistently:
  NIC efficiency eta=0.65
  distributed efficiency delta=0.70
  target utilization u=0.70
  effective c6in.32xlarge one-direction capacity
    = (200e9/8)*eta*delta*u = 7,962,500,000 bytes/s = 63.7 Gbps.
These factors are assumptions. Do not apply another 70% headroom after this.
For a strictly single-machine party one could omit distributed efficiency,
but the main comparison deliberately uses the same factors at every scale.

Let X_p(N) and Y_p(N) be the COMPLETE bytes sent and received per query by
party p, including amortized malicious preprocessing, checking, delivery of
garbled material, online input/output, and required local-cluster routing.
For R=10000 and h=$7.2576/hour:
  K_p = max(1, ceil(R*max(X_p,Y_p) / 7,962,500,000)).
  EC2_USD/year = 8760*h*sum_p K_p.
If all ten parties are symmetric, this is 10*K*8760*h.
If using an average-party byte count, apply a justified hottest-party factor
before K; do not assume that symmetric byte distribution follows from a low
total-communication protocol. Include required delivery copies explicitly.

4. SSD CAPACITY: PERSISTENT STATE AND ROLLING MATERIAL ARE DIFFERENT
Let S_p(N) be persistent garbled/encoded memory state at party p, including
the actual ORAM/cuckoo/position-map layout, labels, authentication, and
independent state replicas. Let C_p(N) be FRESH stored garbled/preprocessed
material consumed per query at party p. Let T_p be the buffer horizon in
seconds, and Q_p the extra scratch/working storage. Then:
  provisioned_SSD_bytes_p = S_p(N) + R*T_p*C_p(N) + Q_p.
  SSD_capacity_USD/year = 12*0.08 * sum_p(provisioned_SSD_bytes_p/2^30).
Round actual EBS allocations to volume GiB and retain desired free space.
An explicit persistent state replica factor belongs in S; the NIC machine
count alone does not prove that every machine needs a complete state copy.

Material produced per query C_p is generally NOT X_p. Malicious multiparty
garbling may communicate many shares/checks/ciphertext contributions while
storing only the resulting tables. Conversely, each evaluator might store
a full garbled table while a garbler stores only its local shares. Count real
roles and multiplicities rather than blindly multiplying everything by ten.

The one-minute buffer illustration below assumes, ONLY for unit sensitivity,
C_p = complete MB/query/party and ten parties each store their own copy:
 C MB/query  rolling GiB/party (60 sec)    SSD capacity USD/year (all 10)
     1                 558.79                         5,364.42
    10                5587.94                        53,644.18
   100               55879.35                       536,441.80
  1000              558793.54                     5,364,418.03
A longer buffer multiplies capacity linearly; it does not increase steady
production capacity. At 10,000 QPS, fresh material must still be generated and
checked for 315.36 billion queries/year. Precomputing before service launch
shifts production in time; it does not make its CPU or communication free.
Do not divide the cost of one ordinary garbled circuit across arbitrary
queries, reuse its consumed labels/correlations, or assume a reusable-garbling
theorem without a concrete supported construction. Persistent encoded memory
and permitted state transitions are not the same as reusing consumed tables.

5. N=2^28 VERSUS N=2^32 CAPACITY
The flat 64N-byte key/value database is 16 GiB and 256 GiB, respectively.
Define e_p(N)=S_p(N)/(64N). This includes the ACTUAL garbled storage expansion
and any state replication at that party. If e is constant and each party
stores one equally sized state, annual ten-party capacity cost is:
  N=2^28: $153.60 * e
  N=2^32: $2457.60 * e.
This 16x size increase applies to persistent storage when expansion is fixed;
it is not automatically a 16x increase in garbled work or bytes/query.

Illustrative storage multipliers, not claims about a specific garbled RAM:
 e       GiB/party at2^28   GiB/party at2^32   ten-party annual USD (2^28;2^32)
 1              16              256                    153.60;       2457.60
 128          2048            32768                  19660.80;     314572.80
 768         12288           196608                 117964.80;    1887436.80
e=128 corresponds to one active 128-bit label per bit of flat stored data;
e=768 further multiplies that hypothetical representation by six. A garbling
with arithmetic words, vector labels, different tree expansion, both wire
labels, or other state representations has different e. Do not assign these
illustrative values to PicoGRAM or multiparty arithmetic garbling unverified.

7. HOW TO USE THESE NUMBERS IN THE CONSTRUCTION COMPARISON
First derive S_p(N), C_p(N), X_p(N), Y_p(N), and independent state/producer
counts from the selected malicious-secure construction. Then apply the SSD
capacity and network formulas separately. Include setup, fresh-material
production, verification, online work, and output release before quoting a
total; a two-party semi-honest garbling table-size number is not already the
cost of ten-party malicious garbling with abort. Show SSD throughput as an
explicit nonbinding assumption, supported by the actual provisioned topology,
rather than using SSD residence to erase ongoing preprocessing or network load.

8. ROLE-AWARE SINGLE-EVALUATOR REFINEMENT (2026-10-13)
The backend review's corrected per-AND table contribution is 192 bytes per
garbler, with nine garblers and one evaluator. The evaluator receives
9*192=1728 bytes per AND. The earlier 128.5-byte interpretation was incorrect:
the additive share of the selector bit is a field share, not a packed bit.
These protocol constants come from the concurrent backend review; keep them
synchronized with its final choice of preprocessing formula and security model.

Let A be AND gates/query and let P be average per-party preprocessing bytes
sent per AND, with matching average receive demand. The current finite-packing
paper-leading-cost proxy is P=7113.6 bytes (71,136 bytes globally), across all
ten parties. This uses outer ell=5,c=2 and SuperPack k=3,t_SP=5,epsilon_SP=.4
covering four actual corruptions. Repairing the source's multiplication-count
algebra gives P=7389.6 (73,896 bytes globally) as a separate sensitivity. The
earlier P=6204.8 value applies the formal unified epsilon=.5 expression without
an integral exact backend packing; it is not the current finite central case.
Sublinear VOLE-generator/PCG production cost is still additional to these
paper-leading-cost coefficients and must not be treated as measured or free.
The source only gives an aggregate; uniform per-party transmit/receive load
is a stated scheduling assumption, not a theorem supplied by that formula.
Inputs, outputs, fixed check terms, and any formula-repair sensitivity must be
added to P before applying these expressions. Use h=1.12 as the same hot-party
sizing proxy used for interactive MPC. Then, for a fixed evaluator:
  garbler tx = h*P + 192; garbler rx = h*P;
  evaluator tx = h*P; evaluator rx = h*P + 1728.
Multiply by A and 10,000 queries/s, and size each role by its own max(tx,rx).
  k_g = ceil(10000*A*(h*P+192)/7,962,500,000)
  k_e = ceil(10000*A*(h*P+1728)/7,962,500,000)
  total EC2 instances = 9*k_g + k_e
  EC2 USD/year = (9*k_g+k_e)*7.2576*8760.
Require at least one instance for each role when A=0 is used in a unit case.
Do not multiply the evaluator's node requirement by all ten parties.

For P=7113.6 and h=1.12, hP=7967.232 bytes/AND. Garbler demand is 8159.232
and evaluator demand 9695.232 bytes/AND. Ignoring integer rounding, the sum
of the ten role capacities is 83,128.32 bytes/AND, whereas ten evaluator-sized
roles would reserve 96,952.32. A hot-party sizing factor is not an extra
transmitted message: raw billed sends per AND are 10*P + 9*192 = 72,864 bytes
under the stated protocol-count interpretation, before framing or extra terms.

If evaluators are uniformly assigned across ten independent valid garbled-RAM
states, every physical party is evaluator for one tenth of queries and garbler
for nine tenths. Average table transmit and receive both become 172.8 bytes/AND
per party; capacity per party becomes hP+172.8. This uses both NIC directions
and can save about 2.08% in the continuous full model, before rounding. In a
table-only model the gain could approach twofold, but preprocessing dominates
the present constants. Rotating the evaluator is not assumed to be a free
handoff of one mutable state: charge independent states and their initialization,
or establish an appropriate state-transfer protocol. Latency/concurrency needs
may require more states than the minimum number for role balancing.

9. ROLLING TABLES VERSUS A COMPLETE GARBLED EPOCH
The rolling-buffer formula in Section 4 is conditional on the chosen scheme
supporting generation, validation, and safe consumption in that streaming
schedule. SSD residence itself does not establish this property. A construction
may require a complete epoch/circuit, labels, or verification commitments before
evaluation starts. In that case, if one epoch serves E queries and has M_epoch
bytes of garbled/preprocessed material at a role, provision the required epoch
material and any concurrently prefetched next epoch, not an arbitrary 60-second
buffer smaller than the protocol permits.

For L concurrent independent states with equal epochs, a conservative capacity
budget is persistent_state_bytes + L*M_epoch + prefetched_next_epoch_bytes
+ scratch. A peak full-epoch requirement is distinct from average used bytes;
provisioned EBS capacity is charged until released and volumes cannot simply
be billed as a linearly shrinking file. If an authenticated streaming schedule
is established, the R*T*C rolling formula becomes appropriate. Whichever
schedule is used, verification must complete before a dependent output release,
fresh material cannot be reused, and lifetime generation/communication must
cover every served query. The AWS model should expose epoch length/buffering
as a construction parameter rather than silently treating it as a storage knob.
