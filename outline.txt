

Section 1: Status Quo of Private Blockchain Access 

In our previous project with Ethereum Foundation, we developed
a TEE-based oblivious access server 
that enables private accesses to on-chain data. 

This proposal will explore methods to remove the TEE and still obtain
logarithmic scaling. 


Ethereum Foundation recently implemented 
a private block access
service based on a GPU-accelerated 
version of the InsPIRe Private Information Retrieval scheme.
