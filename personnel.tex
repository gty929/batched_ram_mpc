\paragraph{PI: Afonso Tinoco.}
Afonso Tinoco
is the PI of the proposed project. He is a co-founder of Oblivious Labs, Inc. and a
Ph.D.\ student at CMU. His research focuses on oblivious computation.
%Before pursuing his Ph.D., Tinoco worked as a Software Engineer at
%SingleStore, contributing significantly to the storage and replication
%layers of a distributed database system. 
%Tinoco obtained his bachelor's and
%master's degrees at the University of Lisbon -- Instituto Superior Tecnico.
Tinoco was the captain and co-founder of Lisbon cybersecurity CTF team
(2015-2023). Tinoco has demonstrated exceptional problem-solving skills and
algorithmic proficiency through his participation in both CTF and
competitive programming international finals, including CSAW CTF
(2018,2019,2021,2023 (2nd place)), European CyberSecurity Challenge (2021),
Hack In The Box Pro CTF (2021), RuCTF (2018,2019), VolgaCTF (2017), ACM
ICPC Regional SWERC (2015, 2016), and IOI (2012, 2013, 2014).

Tinoco will be working full-time at Oblivious Labs, Inc.\ during the
performance period, and the proposed project will be his primary project.

\paragraph{Key personnel: Elaine Shi.}
Shi is a Professor at CMU, an ACM fellow and an IACR fellow. 
%She previously
%took one semester of leave from CMU partly to help with Oblivious Labs'
%technical and business developments. 
Shi has won numerous awards for her
work on oblivious computation, including a Packard Fellowship, a Sloan
Fellowship, an ONR YIP award, the ACM CCS Test-of-Time award, the NSA Best
Scientific Cybersecurity Paper, Top Picks in Hardware and Embedded
Security, ASPLOS best paper award, and others. Shi will be working
part-time on the proposed project.

\paragraph{Research engineer: Tianyao Gu (subject to CPT approval).}
We expect the research engineer for the proposed project to be Tianyao Gu,
subject to Curricular Practical Training (CPT) approval. Gu is a co-founder
of Oblivious Labs, Inc. and a Ph.D.\
student at CMU, conducting research on oblivious computation and applied
cryptography. Gu has been a main contributor to our open source
libraries~\cite{olabs-okv,ostl} and several publications on this
topic~\cite{flexosort,picogram}, and he is a co-author of
Zebra~\cite{zebra} and our CRYPTO~2026 paper~\cite{mparithgarble}, on which
our Garbled RAM work builds. Gu will be working part-time on the proposed
project.
