\paragraph{Task 1: Faster Garbled RAM (80\% of the overall effort)}
\begin{itemize}
\item {\bf Milestone 1.a: extending Garbled RAM to multiple parties.}
We will combine the multi-party arithmetic garbling techniques of our
CRYPTO~2026 paper~\cite{mparithgarble} with the RAM techniques of PicoGRAM
and Zebra, initially targeting a setting with 10--20 parties
(\Cref{sec:gram}).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Expected number of hours.} 182.2 personnel-hours (Tinoco: 115,
Shi: 19.2, research engineer: 48).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Budget.} \$13,004.85 (Tinoco: \$8,159.25, Shi: \$1,440.00,
research engineer: \$3,405.60), not including fringe benefits.

\item {\bf Milestone 1.b: faster preprocessing.}
We will improve the preprocessing 
of our Garbled RAM scheme (e.g., based on Ring-LPN 
rather than LPN), and create an open-source implementation.
%We will replace the LPN-based pseudorandom correlation generators in our
%preprocessing with Ring-LPN-based ones~\cite{ringlpnpcg}, and adapt and
%benchmark them in our framework, so that preprocessing is no longer an
%end-to-end bottleneck.

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Expected number of hours.} 182.2 personnel-hours (Tinoco: 115,
Shi: 19.2, research engineer: 48).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Budget.} \$13,004.85 (Tinoco: \$8,159.25, Shi: \$1,440.00,
research engineer: \$3,405.60), not including fringe benefits.

\item {\bf Milestone 1.c: GPU and AVX-512 acceleration.}
We will accelerate garbling and preprocessing on GPUs and with AVX-512
vector instructions.

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Expected number of hours.} 181.2 personnel-hours (Tinoco: 114,
Shi: 19.2, research engineer: 48).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Budget.} \$12,933.90 (Tinoco: \$8,088.30, Shi: \$1,440.00,
research engineer: \$3,405.60), not including fringe benefits.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Task 2: MicrORAM (20\% of the overall effort)}
\begin{itemize}
\item {\bf Milestone 2.a: develop a full-featured implementation.}
We will complete our work-in-progress implementation of MicrORAM and
deliver a production-quality library that supports both small and large
entries (\Cref{sec:microram}).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Expected number of hours.} 45.8 personnel-hours (Tinoco: 29,
Shi: 4.8, research engineer: 12).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Budget.} \$3,268.95 (Tinoco: \$2,057.55, Shi: \$360.00,
research engineer: \$851.40), not including fringe benefits.

\item {\bf Milestone 2.b: proof of security.}
We will work on proving the security of our new MicrORAM scheme.

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Expected number of hours.} 45.8 personnel-hours (Tinoco: 29,
Shi: 4.8, research engineer: 12).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Budget.} \$3,268.95 (Tinoco: \$2,057.55, Shi: \$360.00,
research engineer: \$851.40), not including fringe benefits.

\item {\bf Milestone 2.c: tailoring MicrORAM to Ethereum's application.}
We will integrate MicrORAM into our oblivious key-value store for Ethereum
state and Merkle proofs, test it on both the account dataset and the full
Ethereum state, including the retrieval of Merkle paths, and use it to
upgrade the oblivious server that we delivered to the Ethereum Foundation
in our earlier grant.

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Expected number of hours.} 44.8 personnel-hours (Tinoco: 28,
Shi: 4.8, research engineer: 12).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent {\it Budget.} \$3,198.00 (Tinoco: \$1,986.60, Shi: \$360.00,
research engineer: \$851.40), not including fringe benefits.
\end{itemize}
